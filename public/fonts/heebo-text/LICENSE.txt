Modified: the hebrew, latin and symbols subsets of public/fonts/heebo-v28 were
instantiated at weight 500 and merged into one static TrueType file. It is used
only for the invisible text layer of the report PDF (app/lib/report-pdf-text.ts):
the glyphs are never painted, the file gives a viewer the characters and their widths.
